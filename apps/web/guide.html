நிறுவுங்கள்Install Mozhi

பதிவிறக்கி, Applications-க்கு இழுத்து விடுங்கள்.

Mac: open the .dmg and drag Mozhi into Applications. The first time, right-click Mozhi → Open → Open. It's an unsigned beta, so macOS asks once.

Windows: run the setup .exe. If SmartScreen warns, click More info → Run anyway.

Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 12+, or Windows 10/11 (64-bit).

அனுமதி கொடுங்கள்Allow microphone and Accessibility

கேட்க மைக்ரோஃபோன், ஒட்ட Accessibility.

Mozhi asks for two things: the microphone, so it can hear you, and Accessibility, so it can notice the double-tap and paste for you. Windows only needs the microphone.

It listens only while the pill is open, never records your typing, and nothing leaves your computer.

Updating on a Mac? Remove the old Mozhi under Privacy & Security → Accessibility and add the new one.

பேச்சு மாதிரியைப் பெறுங்கள்Download a speech model

ஒரு முறை பதிவிறக்குங்கள். பிறகு இணையம் தேவையில்லை.

On first launch Mozhi offers the recommended model, Tamil Small, trained on Tamil by IIT Madras (about 265 MB). Download it once and Mozhi works offline from then on.

Speak lots of English, or heavy Tanglish? Large v3 Turbo keeps English words in English letters. Switch any time under Models.

இருமுறை தட்டிப் பேசுங்கள்Double-tap Control and speak

எழுத வேண்டிய இடத்தில் கிளிக் செய்து, Control-ஐ இருமுறை தட்டுங்கள்.

Click where you want to type, in any app. Double-tap ⌃ Control (Ctrl on Windows). The pill rises at the bottom of your screen and names the app it will type into, so text never lands in the wrong window.

Then just talk: Tamil, Tanglish, English, or all three in one sentence. Your words appear as you speak.

சேர்த்திடுங்கள்Insert it

மீண்டும் இருமுறை தட்டுங்கள், அல்லது Enter.

Double-tap Control again, or press ↵ Enter. Mozhi finishes the last few words, pastes the text where your cursor was, and puts your old clipboard back.

  • ⌃⌃ or ↵ insert
  • ⇧↵ new line in the pill
  • esc cancel and throw the audio away

பேசும்போதே திருத்துங்கள்Edit while you speak

Cursor எங்கே இருக்கிறதோ, அங்கே அடுத்த சொற்கள் விழும்.

Click into the text in the pill while Mozhi is still listening. Fix a name, add a line: every phrase you say next goes in where your cursor is. The words it's still hearing show on a live line underneath.

எழுத்து முறையைத் தேர்ந்தெடுங்கள்Choose how it writes

ஒரே பேச்சு, நான்கு வடிவம்.

Click the chip in the pill, or pick from the menu-bar icon:

  • Auto picks Tamil or English for each sentence.
  • தமிழ் writes Tamil script.
  • Tanglish writes Tamil in English letters.
  • English writes English.

பிழை வந்தால்If something goes wrong

உங்கள் பதிவு பத்திரமாக இருக்கும்.

Mozhi keeps your recording. Try again transcribes it once more and lets you check before inserting. Copy text keeps what you have. Report opens a GitHub issue with your version and the error; nothing you dictated is included, and nothing is sent until you submit it.

No sound reaching Mozhi? Check the Microphone permission. Text not pasting? Check Accessibility.

௰

உங்களுக்கு ஏற்றபடிMake it yours

மெனு bar-இல் உள்ள ஃ ஐகானிலிருந்து Mozhi-ஐத் திறங்கள்.

  • Shortcut: another key (right Control, Option or Fn), the gap between taps, and “pause to edit before inserting”.
  • Writing: live preview, voice emoji, restoring your clipboard, and optional local AI polish through Ollama.
  • History: everything you dictated, kept on your computer. Turn it off any time.
  • Home: open Mozhi when you log in, so the double-tap is always ready.
